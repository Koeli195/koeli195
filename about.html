ABOUT

I’m KOELI, a multi-instrumentalist, music producer and mixing & mastering engineer based in Geneva, Switzerland.

I’ve been creating, recording and producing music for years, working across guitars, bass, drums, synthesizers and vocals. My own work is primarily rooted in electronic music, pop and R&B, while also exploring indie and acoustic styles.

I work with artists to develop their sound, from production and arrangement through to mixing and mastering. My approach is focused on creating music that feels honest, powerful and intentional.

Outside of music, I hold a PhD in physics. My scientific background informs my approach to sound, combining technical precision with an understanding of how sound works.
